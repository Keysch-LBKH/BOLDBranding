Review & wrap-up
A few final questions to turn this into action.
1. What did you learn about yourself or your business while doing this exercise?
2. Did anything surprise you?
3. What could you...
4. What is essential for this business — the thing it can never compromise on?
5. What must this business avoid — the thing that would break trust or go against its values?
6. In 3 years, fully aligned with these values, what does success look like?
7. Make a promise to yourself: what's one action you can take based on what you learned about yourself and your business, and when are you going to do it by?
